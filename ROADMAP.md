# Roadmap

Ideas for what Savings Planner could do next. The site shows this page under **Roadmap** in the footer, so anyone can see what's coming.

To add an idea, edit this file (on GitHub: the pencil icon) and add a line under **Ideas**:

    - **Name of the idea** — what it would do and why it's useful.

Move it up to **Planned** once it's decided, and when it ships it goes in the changelog instead. The site picks up changes the next time it's deployed.

## Planned

- **Tutorial mode** — a guided introduction for first-time users. To be described: what it should walk through, and how.
- **State pension triple lock changes** — the government has proposed changing how the triple lock uprates the state pension. Waiting for a bill to set out the details; model the new rule then, with a setting to compare it against the current triple lock. An earlier draft is in #34.

## Ideas

- **Left to heirs** — what passes to your heirs after the second death in couple mode (or yours, planning alone), net of inheritance tax, including the nil-rate band a spouse can pass on and the home allowance. The estate snapshot already records what's needed.
- **Chart by person** — in couple mode, stack the chart by partner instead of by pot.
- **Debt owners** — mark each debt as yours, your partner's or joint, so a death settles it from the right estate.
- **Marry at age X** — in couple mode, see what marrying or forming a civil partnership would change: inheritance tax, ISAs, Marriage Allowance.
- **Moving investments between spouses** — rebalance the GIA between a married couple (no capital gains tax between spouses) to use both people's allowances and tax bands.
- **Joint-life annuities** — an annuity that keeps paying the survivor.
